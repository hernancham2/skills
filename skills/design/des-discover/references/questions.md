# Design discovery question bank

Pick between 12 and 18, depending on the project's gaps. Those marked ★ give the most information if there is only time for one per block.

## 1. Business and positioning

- ★ If a customer had to explain in one sentence why they choose you over someone else, what would they say?
- Who are the three closest competitors or alternatives? Where do you want to resemble them and where not?
- What perception do people have of the brand today and what would you like them to have two years from now?
- Is there something in your sector that everyone does visually and that you want to avoid (or must respect to be credible)?

## 2. Audience and context of use

- ★ Describe the person who will use this the most: approximate age, trade, how comfortable they are with technology.
- Where and how do they use it: seated at a monitor, standing with a phone, wearing gloves, in the sun, in a hurry?
- How long do they use it at a stretch: seconds, minutes, the whole workday?
- Are there users with low vision, color blindness, motor difficulties or who use a screen reader? Is accessibility a legal or contractual obligation?
- Which languages must it support? Does any of them read right to left or use another alphabet?
- Which devices and screen sizes are typical? Are there old devices or slow connections?

## 3. Personality

- ★ If the brand were a person, how would it speak and how would it dress? And how would it not?
- Place the brand on each pair (1 to 5): formal / friendly · sober / expressive · classic / contemporary · serious / playful · exclusive / accessible · dense / spacious · discreet / eye-catching.
- Give me three words that should come to mind when seeing the product, and three that never should.
- What emotion should someone feel on first use? And when something goes wrong?

## 4. Existing brand

- ★ What of what already exists cannot be touched, and who decided that?
- Do you have the logo in a vector format (SVG, AI, EPS)? Who designed it and who has the originals?
- Is there a brand manual? Is it followed in practice?
- Which typefaces do you use today and under what license? Does it cover use on the web and in apps?
- What do you like least about how it looks today? And what do customers recognize immediately?
- Are the name and the logo registered?

## 5. References

- ★ Show me three digital products that you think are well made. What exactly about each one?
- Show me three that you do not like. What exactly?
- Is there a brand outside software whose style you admire?
- Among your competitors, which one looks best and why?

## 6. Product and platforms

- ★ Where will this live: web, iOS, Android, desktop, physical screens, email, print?
- What type of screens predominate: forms, data-heavy tables, long reading, image catalog, dashboards with charts?
- Is dark mode needed? As a preference or because of the usage environment?
- Must it coexist visually with another product, with a client's system or with a platform (app store, intranet)?
- Will there be white-labeling or different themes per client?

## 7. Constraints

- ★ What interface technology is already decided (framework, component library, third-party design system)?
- Is there budget for paid fonts, illustration, photography or custom icons?
- What level of accessibility is required (for example, WCAG 2.2 AA)? Who will verify it?
- What is the date for the first visible version and what must be defined by then?
- Who will maintain the guidelines afterwards: is there a designer on the team or only developers?

## 8. Content and voice

- ★ How is the user addressed: formally or informally? Show me a real message you consider well written.
- How should an error message sound? And a confirmation of something important?
- Are there business terms that must always be used the same way? Forbidden words?
- Who writes the interface texts?
- Which date, currency and number formats are used?

## 9. Decision and success

- ★ Who approves the design and by what criterion? Is it one person or a committee?
- Who else will have an opinion even though they do not decide?
- How will we know the design worked: fewer errors, more conversions, less support, brand recognition?
- How many review rounds are expected?
